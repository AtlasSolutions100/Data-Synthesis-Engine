CRITICAL ARCHITECTURE REQUIREMENT — SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH (SSOT)
This is extremely important and must be strictly followed across the entire system:
There needs to be a Single Source of Truth (SSOT). Every number is entered just once at the transactional level. The software automatically links, "rolls up," and pushes this exact data to the client profile and the Profit & Loss (P&L) report.
Here is exactly how this connected architecture works across the workflow:
- The Single Source of Truth: Every time  an invoice, record an expense, or pay a bill, the transaction is logged here. This is the origin point.
- The "Roll Up" (Subledgers to Control Accounts): A roll-up is the automatic grouping of detailed data into broader categories. For example, dozens of individual client transactions (the subledger) are automatically grouped (rolled up) into your Accounts Receivable or Revenue accounts in the General Ledger.
- The Client Level: Because the software is connected, when you look at an individual Client's Ledger (or customer account), you see a filtered view of only their specific transactions.
- The P&L (Profit and Loss): This statement pulls data from the aggregated revenue and expense accounts in your GL. It sums up all the rolled-up client totals and operational costs to show your net profit for a specific period.
All changes in this task must respect and reinforce this SSOT architecture. Data should never be duplicated manually — everything must flow automatically from the transactional/GL level.
